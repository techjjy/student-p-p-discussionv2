# Peer Case Rounds (Voice)

A spoken small-group case discussion for fourth-year medical students. You join three simulated classmates who talk out loud, each in their own voice — **Priya** (analytical and diagnostic), **Marcus** (jargon-dense and efficient) and **Devon** (empathetic and ethical) — to discuss a five-month-old infant with fever and a rash.

Together you discuss the **symptoms**, the **differential diagnosis**, the **cause**, the **medical tests** to order, the **treatment** and the **prognosis**.

## What you need

- **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on a computer (other browsers can play the voices but usually cannot use the microphone; you can always type instead).
- A microphone, and ideally **headphones**, so the microphone does not pick up your classmates' voices.

## How to use it

1. Open the page. Press **🔊 Test the voices** and **🎤 Test my microphone**. When the browser asks for the microphone, choose **Allow**.
2. Type your first name and the access code from your instructor, and press **Join the discussion**.
3. Your classmates speak. With **Hands-free** on, the microphone switches on by itself when they finish; just talk, then pause for about two seconds and your answer is sent. With Hands-free off, press **🎤 Speak**, talk, press **■ Done**, check the text and press **Send**. You can also type.
4. Open **Case file** at any time. Test results appear only when the group orders a test.
5. **⏸ Hold** (or say "hold") pauses everything: voices stop and the microphone switches off. **▶ Continuation** picks it up again.
6. Say or type **stop** to finish. You receive feedback on your **preciseness**, **explanations** and **constructiveness**, and tips to improve. Press **🔊 Read evaluation aloud** to hear it, and **Download transcript and evaluation** to keep a copy.

*For educational use only. The patient is fictional. In Chrome and Edge, speech recognition is processed by the browser maker's online speech service.*
