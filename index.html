Case file: Baby Girl A. Reyes-Whitfield, 5 months

Chief complaint

Brought by her biological mother: high fever for more than 4 days, feeding poorly, and a rash over her body.

History of present illness

Fever began about 5 days ago; now continuous at 39.2–40.1 °C (102.5–104.1 °F), poorly responsive to alternating acetaminophen and ibuprofen. Rash for 2 days: blotchy, pink-red, flat to slightly raised, non-blistering; trunk first, then arms, legs and diaper area. Eyes "pink and glassy" for 2 days, no discharge or crusting. Lips red and slightly cracked. Very fussy, hard to console, high-pitched crying, fragmented sleep. Taking about half her usual feeds; 3–4 wet diapers in 24 hours. Hands and feet "puffy and warm" today. No cough, congestion, vomiting or diarrhea. No new soaps, foods or medicines. No travel. Small in-home daycare twice weekly; no known sick contacts.

Urgent care on fever day 3: rapid strep antigen negative; urinalysis reported "no infection" (details not given). Sent home with reassurance.

Background

Mixed race (Filipino mother, Irish-American father). Term, 39 weeks, uncomplicated vaginal delivery, 3.3 kg; no NICU. Normal development and newborn screens. Vaccines up to date through 4 months (HepB ×2, DTaP, IPV, Hib, PCV13, rotavirus ×2); none in past 2 weeks. No known allergies. Healthy parents; no autoimmune, rheumatologic, vasculitic or recurrent-fever family history. Lives with both parents and a healthy 3-year-old sibling. Smoke-free; no pets, ticks or untreated water. Breast milk plus formula; no solids yet.

Vital signs

Temp 39.8 °C rectal · HR 168 · RR 36 · BP 88/54 · SpO₂ 98% room air · weight 6.9 kg (~45th percentile).

Examination

Irritable, intermittently consolable, mildly ill, not toxic. Bilateral bulbar conjunctival injection, non-exudative, limbal sparing. Lips erythematous, dry, slightly cracked; oropharynx mildly erythematous. One small mobile, mildly tender right cervical node (~1 cm). Diffuse blanching maculopapular rash on trunk, extremities and perineum; no vesicles, petechiae or purpura. Mild edema of dorsal hands and feet, warm; no periungual peeling; no joint swelling. Tachycardic, regular, no murmur. Lungs clear. Abdomen soft, no organomegaly. Moves all limbs; anterior fontanelle soft and flat.
